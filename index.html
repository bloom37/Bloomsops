Front Desk SOPs — Reception
02 — Reception
Front Desk SOPs
Opening the Studio
Arrive 20 minutes before first class
  1. Disarm alarm, turn on all lights, and adjust thermostat to studio standard.
  2. Walk the entire studio — check reformers, mats, and props for cleanliness and alignment. Wipe down any equipment from the night before.
  3. Check restrooms: restock paper, soap, empty trash if needed.
  4. Open Arketa on the front desk device. Review the day's schedule, class sizes, and any notes flagged from the previous shift.
  5. Check the waitlist in Arketa — confirm auto-enroll worked correctly. Manually contact waitlisted clients if a spot opened after the notification window.
  6. Prepare the check-in area: tissues, pens, sign-in sheet (if used as backup), and any retail display.
  7. Unlock the front door exactly 15 minutes before the first class start time — not before.
If you arrive and something is broken, damaged, or unsafe — do not open. Text the manager and owner immediately.
Client Check-In
Every client, every time
  1. Greet by name when possible. "Hi [name], welcome back!" for returning clients. "Welcome to Bloom!" for new clients.
  2. Check them in on Arketa immediately — never let clients check themselves in unless a self-serve kiosk is set up.
  3. For new clients: have them complete the waiver in Arketa (digital). Do not allow them in the studio without a completed waiver.
  4. For new clients: give a 60-second orientation — where to store shoes/bags, where equipment is, intro to the instructor.
  5. If a client walks in without a reservation, check Arketa for open spots. If class is full, offer the waitlist or the next available class.
  6. If a client's membership is expired or has no credits — let them know warmly, offer to purchase on the spot. Do not let them take class without valid booking or payment.
Never tell a client "don't worry about it" and wave them through without a booking. Every client in the room must be accounted for in Arketa.
Phone, Email & DM Inquiries
  1. Answer all calls within 3 rings. Greet: "Thank you for calling Bloom Pilates, this is [your name], how can I help you?"
  2. Respond to emails and DMs within 2 business hours during studio operating hours.
  3. For new client inquiries: gather their name, what they're looking for, and their schedule. Offer to book a trial class directly in Arketa while on the call.
  4. Pricing questions: direct to the pricing page or provide the current rate card. Do not offer undocumented discounts.
  5. If you don't know the answer — say "Let me find that out for you and follow up shortly" and get back to them within 1 hour.
Closing the Studio
  1. Confirm all clients have exited the studio floor.
  2. Wipe down all reformers, mats, rings, and blocks with approved disinfectant spray.
  3. Reset all equipment to starting position — straps, footbars, springs to neutral.
  4. Check restrooms — wipe surfaces, restock, empty trash if over halfway full.
  5. Lock front door. Turn off all lights except any required security lighting.
  6. Set the alarm and confirm it is armed before leaving.
  7. Log any incidents, complaints, or follow-up items in the shift notes.
The last person to leave is responsible for the close checklist — even if an instructor is still there. Do not assume someone else handled it.
Payments & Memberships
  1. All transactions are processed through Arketa. Do not accept cash unless a cash policy is defined by ownership.
  2. When selling memberships or class packs, always confirm the correct product is selected in Arketa before processing payment.
  3. If a payment fails — inform the client privately. Offer to update the card in Arketa. Do not allow repeated failed payments to accumulate.
  4. Refund requests: log them. Do not process without manager or owner approval.
  5. End-of-day: confirm daily sales total in Arketa matches expected transactions. Flag any discrepancies to the manager.

Instructor SOPs — Teaching Staff
03 — Teaching Staff
Instructor SOPs
Before Class
  1. Arrive minimum 15 minutes before class start time — no exceptions. If you will be late, notify the manager immediately.
  2. Review the class roster in Arketa: note new clients, returning clients with injuries on file, and class size.
  3. Set up the studio for your class format — springs, footbars, props positioned and ready before clients enter.
  4. Prepare your playlist or confirm audio is working. Silence your personal phone.
  5. Greet clients as they arrive. For new clients, introduce yourself and ask about any injuries or limitations before class begins.
Know your roster. If you see a new name you don't recognize, verify they're checked in and have completed their waiver before class starts.
During Class — Standards
  1. Start class no more than 2 minutes after scheduled time — out of respect for clients who were punctual.
  2. Do a full verbal health check at the start: "Any injuries or areas of concern today? This is your time to let me know."
  3. Offer modifications for every exercise. No client should ever feel unable to participate.
  4. Do not use your phone during class for any reason other than a genuine emergency.
  5. Maintain professional boundaries — compliments are encouraged; personal conversations should happen before or after class, not during instruction.
  6. If a client appears to be in pain or discomfort — stop. Check in with them. If there is any concern about injury, they stop exercising that day.
  7. End class on time. If running short, skip extra content — never run over into the next class's setup window.
After Class
  1. Stay in the studio for at least 5 minutes post-class to answer questions and connect with clients.
  2. Assist in wiping down equipment if front desk is not yet present — Bloom's clean studio is everyone's responsibility.
  3. Return all props and springs to neutral/storage position.
  4. Log any client concerns, injuries observed, or incidents into the shift notes or directly to the manager.
  5. If a client mentioned an injury or expressed frustration — flag it to the manager immediately, do not wait until next shift.
Schedule, Subs & Absences
  1. You are responsible for your scheduled classes. If you cannot teach, you are responsible for finding a qualified sub — notify the manager simultaneously.
  2. Sub requests must be submitted minimum 48 hours in advance for non-emergencies.
  3. Emergency absences (illness, accident): notify the manager by text immediately — do not wait for a call back.
  4. All subs must be approved by the manager or owner before being confirmed to clients.
  5. Do not communicate your absence directly to clients on social media. All client communications go through official Bloom channels.
A class without an instructor is never acceptable. It is your professional responsibility to ensure coverage before you are absent.
Injury & Emergency Protocol
  1. If a client is injured during class — stop class immediately. Assess calmly. Do not minimize or dismiss what the client reports.
  2. If injury appears serious — call 911 first. Then notify the manager or owner.
  3. Know where the first aid kit is located at all times.
  4. Document every incident in writing that day — what happened, who was present, what was said, what action was taken. This is legally important.
  5. Do not post about any incident on social media. Do not discuss it with other clients. All communications go through the owner.

Studio Manager SOPs
04 — Management
Studio Manager SOPs
Weekly Operational Checklist
Monday — Set the Week
Review Arketa schedule — confirm all classes have instructors and no gaps
Flag clients with expiring memberships or packages this week
Review last week's shift notes and follow up on unresolved issues
Ongoing — Throughout the Week
Monitor class fill rates — classes under 30% full 24 hrs out need action
Handle Level 2 escalations within 2 hours
Ensure studio is stocked: cleaning supplies, paper products, retail
Friday — Close the Week
Send weekly summary to owner: attendance, revenue, incidents, one highlight
Confirm weekend staffing is locked and all subs confirmed
Refunds, Waivers & Billing
  1. Late cancel fee waivers: you may waive up to one per client per membership term, at your discretion, for documented extenuating circumstances.
  2. Refund requests under $100: you may approve after reviewing the account history in Arketa and confirming the client's standing.
  3. Refund requests over $100: document and escalate to owner within 24 hours.
  4. All refunds must be processed through Arketa — never issue cash or informal credits.
  5. Any client requesting to cancel their membership: collect their reason, log it, and notify the owner before processing cancellation.
Staff Management
  1. Maintain a master staff availability sheet — updated at least twice per month.
  2. Conduct informal 1:1 check-ins with each staff member at least twice per month — 10 minutes is enough. Log any concerns.
  3. If a staff member is consistently late, violating SOPs, or receiving client complaints — document in writing and notify owner before addressing directly.
  4. New hire onboarding: every new hire must shadow at least 2 full shifts and review this SOP document before being left unsupervised.
  5. Scheduling changes: confirm 2 weeks out minimum, 4 weeks preferred. Communicate changes to affected staff in writing.
Client Experience & Retention
  1. Every new client who completes their first class should receive a follow-up — email or text within 24 hours via Arketa's automated messaging or manually.
  2. Clients who haven't booked in 3+ weeks: flag in Arketa as "at-risk." Ensure a re-engagement message goes out.
  3. Respond to all Google Reviews and social comments within 48 hours — professional, warm, and never defensive.
  4. Collect client feedback quarterly — even a simple 3-question survey sent through Arketa counts.
  5. Note birthdays in client profiles — a personal happy birthday note from Bloom goes a long way.

05 — Owner
Owner SOPs — Andreaa
Monthly Business Review
Pull from Arketa reports on the 1st of each month
  1. Total revenue vs. prior month and vs. goal. Identify the gap — no guessing, look at the numbers.
  2. Total active memberships and class packs. New clients added vs. clients lost (churn). This is your pulse.
  3. Class fill rate by class type. If a class is consistently under 40% full, it needs to be repositioned, promoted harder, or cut.
  4. Top 10 clients by visit frequency — these are your VIPs. Know them. Protect them.
  5. Review all refunds issued that month. Patterns in refund reasons = operational or product problems.
  6. Set 3 priorities for the coming month — written down, shared with manager.
Financial Oversight
  1. Review Arketa revenue reports weekly — takes 10 minutes. You should always know where you are vs. goal.
  2. Reconcile Arketa sales with your bank account monthly. Flag any discrepancies immediately.
  3. Know your fixed costs monthly: rent, payroll, software, insurance. That is your floor. Everything above it is how you build toward freedom.
  4. Any expense over $200 that is not routine: you approve it personally before it is incurred.
  5. Payroll: confirm all instructor hours and any commission structures are documented in writing before each pay period.
Financial freedom starts with knowing your numbers cold. No one should know this business's finances better than you.
Growth & Marketing
  1. Quarterly: evaluate which membership tiers are selling and which aren't. Pricing should be revisited at minimum every 6 months.
  2. Monthly: plan at least one promotional event, intro offer, or community-building activation. Bloom is in year one — growth requires consistent nurturing.
  3. All public-facing promotions and pricing changes must come from you or be explicitly approved by you before going live.
  4. Partnerships: vet any potential partner before committing. Bloom's brand must be protected — align only with businesses that share your values.
  5. Quarterly Google Business Profile audit — ensure hours, photos, and information are accurate.
Legal & Compliance
  1. Ensure all client waivers are current, signed, and stored in Arketa. Audit this quarterly.
  2. Confirm business insurance is active and adequate — covers liability, equipment, and employee injuries. Annual review minimum.
  3. Instructor certifications: maintain a copy on file for every teaching staff member. Calendar renewal dates so you're never caught with an uncertified instructor.
  4. Any incident involving injury: document fully within 24 hours. Retain documentation. Know when to escalate to an attorney.
  5. Review all contractor vs. employee classifications annually — this is a real legal risk area in the fitness industry.
Your law background is a genuine asset here. Use it — and know when the matter is beyond your current knowledge and call in a professional.
